## Limits and retention

These files support arithmetic reproduction from coded records, not full recoding or original-interface replay. Low initial agreement, unclear categories, incomplete captures, prompting differences, personalization and one retained observation remain limitations. Unclear relevance stays in denominators but does not count as yes. P22-Filipino's missing AI Overview is an exception, not an imputed empty panel. Kappa is undefined when there is no category variation and expected agreement is one.

The author reports no human research participants and no institutional ethics review/approval obtained. Permission to analyze supplied exports is confirmed; formal exemption and redistribution rights are not inferred.

Pilot subsets and stale summary CSVs are omitted because full inputs and the script supersede them. The empty evidence-correction CSV contained no correction rows. Paired values, actual decisions and the 12 logic corrections are retained to explain analysis inputs. Submission files, private captures, internal QA, coding-generation helpers and working previews are excluded. Git history preserves earlier files.
